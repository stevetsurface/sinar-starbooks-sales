    Apa itu oversold?What does oversold mean?

    Oversold artinya barang sudah dipesan atau terjual lebih banyak daripada stok fisik yang ada di lokasi itu, sehingga stok tersedia menjadi minus.

    Oversold means more units have been ordered or sold than are physically on hand at that location, so available stock goes negative.

    Contoh: di PAMERAN ASHTA ada 4 Squishy Pao Mix di rak, tetapi 18 sudah dipesan. Tersedia = 4 − 18 = −14. Ke-14 pesanan itu belum bisa dikirim.

    Example: PAMERAN ASHTA has 4 Squishy Pao Mix on the shelf, but 18 are reserved by orders. Available = 4 − 18 = −14. Those 14 orders can't ship.

    Kenapa penting: kalau terlalu lama, marketplace membatalkan pesanan dan toko kena penalti (skor toko turun).

    Why it matters: if they wait too long, the marketplace cancels the orders and the shop is penalised (lower shop score).

    Penyebab umum: stok tidak sinkron antar Shopee/Tokopedia, flash sale atau promo tanggal kembar, dan penjualan pameran yang belum dicatat.

    Common causes: stock not syncing between Shopee and Tokopedia, flash sales or double-date promos, and exhibition sales not entered in time.

    Yang harus dilakukan: transfer stok dari toko lain, percepat penerimaan barang, atau hubungi pembeli sebelum dibatalkan. Lalu cek pengaturan sinkronisasi stok di Jubelio.

    What to do: transfer stock from another store, speed up receiving, or contact the buyer before the order is cancelled. Then check stock sync settings in Jubelio.

    Filter online vs toko (offline)Online vs store (offline) filter

    Tombol Online + toko / Toko (offline) / Online di bawah judul memisahkan penjualan berdasarkan kanal:

    The Online + stores / Stores (offline) / Online switch under the header splits sales by channel:

    • Toko (offline) = transaksi kasir (Jubelio POS) di toko fisik.
    • Online = Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, Lazada, Blibli.
    • Stores (offline) = cashier (Jubelio POS) sales in the physical stores.
    • Online = Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, Lazada, Blibli.

    Penting: pesanan online sering dikirim dari stok toko (mis. PIM atau ASHTA). Di tabel Per toko, pesanan itu masuk kolom Online toko tersebut, bukan Di toko. Contoh 10.10: penjualan kasir normal (Rp 48,7 jt), tetapi online melonjak dan sekitar Rp 56 jt pesanan online dikirim dari toko.

    Important: online orders are often shipped from store stock (e.g. PIM or ASHTA). In the By store table those orders count in that store's Online column, not In-store. On 10.10, cashier sales were normal (Rp 48.7jt) while online spiked, and about Rp 56jt of online orders shipped from stores.

    In the Revenue trend chart, choose Store vs online to see both stacked per day.

    Sumber & pembaruan dataData sources & updates

    • Penjualan & stok: Jubelio akun Wonder Bookstore (termasuk toko marketplace Sinar Starbooks). Waktu pembaruan terlihat di setiap kartu (titik kuning).
    • Data per judul dan keuangan: 90 hari terakhir.
    • Reseller: file Reseller Recency Database & Reseller Tracker Sinar Star, bukan Jubelio.
    • Rencana: data Jubelio ditarik otomatis setiap hari pukul 04.00 WIB ke repo GitHub, lalu dimuat ke dashboard.
    • Sales & stock: the Wonder Bookstore Jubelio account (which includes the Sinar Starbooks marketplace shops). Each card shows when it was last updated (gold dot).
    • Title and finance detail: last 90 days.
    • Resellers: Sinar Star's Reseller Recency Database & Reseller Tracker files, not Jubelio.
    • Planned: Jubelio data pulled automatically every day at 04:00 WIB into a GitHub repo, then loaded into the dashboard.

    Cara pakaiHow to use

    • Periode: pilih rentang atau tanggal sendiri; semua angka dibandingkan dengan periode sebelumnya.
    • Merek dan Online/toko: menyaring Ringkasan, Penjualan dan Keuangan.
    • Excel: tombol hijau di setiap daftar mengunduh file untuk staf (dengan filter kolom).
    • ID / EN: ganti bahasa di kanan atas.
    • Period: pick a range or your own dates; every number is compared with the period before.
    • Brand and Online/stores: filter Overview, Sales and Finance.
    • Excel: the green button on each list downloads a file for staff (with column filters).
    • ID / EN: switch language at the top right.
