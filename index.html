      Ready stock: siap kirim dari gudang dan tokoReady stock: ships now from the warehouse and stores

      –

      Aturan

      1. Stok gudang pusat dihitung penuh. Gudang online dan tiap toko Wonder di Jakarta (Ashta, JKT-TSM, Hublife, PIM) menyimpan 2 eksemplar per judul; sisanya ikut ditawarkan. Toko Bali tidak masuk karena tidak bisa kirim cepat.
      2. Minimal 3 eksemplar tersedia per judul.
      3. Harga retail saja. Judul tanpa harga retail di Accurate tidak masuk.
      4. Tidak masuk: terbitan Gramedia, stok sekolah, voucher, paper bag, kemasan, gift wrap dan paket buku promo.
      5. Gudang pusat dari snapshot Accurate di Drive (tanggalnya tertulis di atas); gudang online dan toko dari Jubelio. Stok berubah tiap hari, jadi file yang dikirim berlaku sampai besoknya; kirim daftar baru tiap hari.
      6. Kirim dari gudang pusat dulu, lalu gudang online. Ambil dari toko hanya kalau keduanya tidak cukup.

      Rules

      1. Central warehouse stock counts in full. The online warehouse and each Jakarta Wonder store (Ashta, JKT-TSM, Hublife, PIM) keep 2 copies of a title; the rest is offered. The Bali stores are left out: they can't ship fast enough.
      2. At least 3 copies available per title.
      3. Retail price only. Titles with no retail price in Accurate are left out.
      4. Left out: Gramedia-published titles, school stock, vouchers, paper bags, packing, gift wrap and promo book bundles.
      5. Central warehouse from the Accurate snapshot on Drive (dated above); the online warehouse and the stores from Jubelio. Stock changes daily, so a file you send is valid until the next day; send a fresh list each day.
      6. Ship from the central warehouse first, then the online warehouse. Pull from a store only when both are short.

      Apa itu oversold?What does oversold mean?

      Oversold artinya barang sudah dipesan atau terjual lebih banyak daripada stok fisik yang ada di lokasi itu, sehingga stok tersedia menjadi minus.

      Oversold means more units have been ordered or sold than are physically on hand at that location, so available stock goes negative.

      Contoh: di PAMERAN ASHTA ada 4 Squishy Pao Mix di rak, tetapi 18 sudah dipesan. Tersedia = 4 − 18 = −14. Ke-14 pesanan itu belum bisa dikirim.

      Example: PAMERAN ASHTA has 4 Squishy Pao Mix on the shelf, but 18 are reserved by orders. Available = 4 − 18 = −14. Those 14 orders can't ship.

      Kenapa penting: kalau terlalu lama, marketplace membatalkan pesanan dan toko kena penalti (skor toko turun).

      Why it matters: if they wait too long, the marketplace cancels the orders and the shop is penalised (lower shop score).

      Penyebab umum: stok tidak sinkron antar Shopee/Tokopedia, flash sale atau promo tanggal kembar, dan penjualan pameran yang belum dicatat.

      Common causes: stock not syncing between Shopee and Tokopedia, flash sales or double-date promos, and exhibition sales not entered in time.

      Yang harus dilakukan: transfer stok dari toko lain, percepat penerimaan barang, atau hubungi pembeli sebelum dibatalkan. Lalu cek pengaturan sinkronisasi stok di Jubelio.

      What to do: transfer stock from another store, speed up receiving, or contact the buyer before the order is cancelled. Then check stock sync settings in Jubelio.

Perlu dipesan ulang: judul yang laku minimal 6 unit dalam 90 hari dan stoknya (tersedia + sudah dipesan) habis sebelum pesanan ulang bisa tiba. KRITIS = habis sebelum pesanan yang dibuat hari ini tiba (cukup kurang dari lead time); SEGERA = kurang dari lead time + 30 hari; PANTAU = kurang dari lead time + 60 hari. KRITIS = runs out before an order placed today can land (cover under the lead time); SEGERA = under lead time + 30 days; PANTAU = under lead time + 60 days. Lead time is measured per supplier from PO to purchase bill (at least 2 POs), otherwise 90 days. Slow movers: stock ≥3 with no sale in 90 days, or ≤2 units with stock ≥15.

      Rapor tokoStore scorecard

      Satu halaman per toko fisik, angka kasir (POS) untuk periode terpilih, dengan peringkat di antara semua toko. Online dari toko = pesanan marketplace yang dikirim dari stok toko itu. Pelanggan terdata dan unit per struk berasal dari data rinci 90 hari, jadi periode lebih panjang dipotong. Stok menipis, stok mati, oversold dan usulan transfer adalah posisi saat ini, bukan periode.

      One page per physical store, POS numbers for the selected period, ranked among all stores. Online from store = marketplace orders shipped from that store's stock. Known customers and units per ticket come from the 90-day order detail, so longer periods are clamped. Low stock, dead stock, oversold and suggested transfers are the current position, not the period.

      Pemulihan pembatalanCancellation recovery

      Tidak semua pembatalan hilang: banyak pembeli yang "ubah pesanan" memesan ulang. Kartu ini mencocokkan ID pelanggan tiap pesanan batal dengan pesanan baru pelanggan yang sama dalam 7 hari. Yang tidak memesan ulang = benar-benar hilang. Judul dan toko pengirim menunjukkan di mana masalahnya: harga, stok kosong, atau pengiriman terlambat.

      Not every cancellation is lost: many "modify order" buyers simply re-order. This card matches each cancelled order's customer ID with a new order by the same customer within 7 days. Those who didn't re-order = truly lost. Titles and shipping stores show where the problem is: price, stock-outs or late shipping.

      Keranjang: online vs tokoBasket: online vs store

      Kartu Keranjang di Ringkasan membandingkan perilaku belanja online dan di toko untuk periode terpilih. Sumbernya data rinci pesanan Jubelio, yang hanya diambil untuk 90 hari terakhir; periode lebih panjang dipotong otomatis.

      The Basket card on the Overview compares shopping behaviour online and in store for the selected period. It uses Jubelio order-level data, which is only pulled for the last 90 days; longer periods are clamped automatically.

      • Transaksi = pesanan / struk yang tidak dibatalkan. Rata-rata per transaksi = pendapatan ÷ transaksi.
      • Unit per keranjang = jumlah unit ÷ transaksi. Kasir Jubelio tidak mengirim qty per baris, jadi tiap baris kasir dihitung 1 unit. Kemasan, kantong, dan materai tidak dihitung.
      • Diskon = diskon penjual + voucher + potongan tambahan yang tercatat di pesanan (bukan subsidi marketplace).
      • Pelanggan terdata = pesanan dengan ID pelanggan. Online hampir selalu terdata; di toko hanya kalau kasir mencatat member/pelanggan (walk-in tidak dihitung). Karena itu angka toko menggambarkan member saja.
      • Pelanggan kembali = pelanggan terdata yang sudah pernah belanja lebih awal dalam 90 hari data. Pembelian sebelum jendela itu tidak terlihat, jadi angka ini sedikit terlalu rendah.
      • Tickets = non-cancelled orders / receipts. Average ticket = revenue ÷ tickets.
      • Units per basket = units ÷ tickets. Jubelio POS doesn't send a quantity per line, so each till line counts as 1 unit. Packaging, bags and stamps are excluded.
      • Discount = seller discount + vouchers + extra deductions recorded on the order (not marketplace-funded subsidies).
      • Known customer = order with a customer ID. Online nearly always has one; in store only when the cashier records a member/customer (walk-ins are not counted), so the store figures describe members only.
      • Returning = a known customer who already had an earlier order within the 90-day data. Purchases before that window aren't visible, so this is slightly understated.

      Sumber & pembaruan dataData sources & updates

      • Planned: Jubelio data pulled automatically every day at 04:00 WIB into a GitHub repo, then loaded into the dashboard.

      Cara pakaiHow to use

      • Periode: pilih rentang atau tanggal sendiri; semua angka dibandingkan dengan periode sebelumnya.
      • Merek dan Online/toko: menyaring Ringkasan, Penjualan dan Keuangan.
      • Excel: tombol hijau di setiap daftar mengunduh file untuk staf (dengan filter kolom).
      • ID / EN: ganti bahasa di kanan atas.
      • Period: pick a range or your own dates; every number is compared with the period before.
      • Brand and Online/stores: filter Overview, Sales and Finance.
      • Excel: the green button on each list downloads a file for staff (with column filters).
      • ID / EN: switch language at the top right.
